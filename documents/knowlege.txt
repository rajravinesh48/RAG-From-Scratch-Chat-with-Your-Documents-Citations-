ACME CORP REMOTE WORK AND VACATION POLICY 2026

1. Vacation Allocation
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. 
Unused vacation days do not roll over to the next year; they expire on December 31st.

2. Remote Work Core Hours
Employees working remotely must be online and reachable during core operating hours. 
The core hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST).

3. Equipment Stipend
New hires receive a one-time home office hardware stipend of $500. 
This stipend can be used to purchase monitors, ergonomic chairs, or keyboards. 
Receipts must be submitted via the internal expense portal within 30 days of purchase.
