LIVING THINGS KNOWLEDGE BASE
Animals, Birds, Pet Animals, Water Animals and Other Living Creatures

============================================================
1. INTRODUCTION TO LIVING THINGS
============================================================

Living things are organisms that carry out basic life processes such as growth,
respiration, nutrition, movement, reproduction, excretion, and response to
their environment.

Examples of living things include:
- Humans
- Animals
- Birds
- Fish
- Insects
- Reptiles
- Amphibians
- Plants
- Fungi
- Microorganisms

Living things need suitable conditions such as food, water, air, and habitat
to survive.

============================================================
2. ANIMALS
============================================================

Animals are living organisms that generally obtain food by eating plants or
other animals. Animals can live on land, in water, in trees, underground, or
in more than one type of habitat.

Major animal groups include:
- Mammals
- Birds
- Reptiles
- Amphibians
- Fish
- Insects
- Arachnids
- Mollusks
- Crustaceans

MAMMALS

Mammals are animals that usually have hair or fur and feed their young with
milk produced by the mother.

Examples:
- Lion
- Tiger
- Elephant
- Dog
- Cat
- Cow
- Horse
- Dolphin
- Whale
- Monkey
- Rabbit
- Goat

Most mammals give birth to live young, although a few mammals, such as the
platypus and echidna, lay eggs.

============================================================
3. WILD ANIMALS
============================================================

Wild animals live naturally in forests, grasslands, deserts, mountains,
wetlands, oceans, and other natural habitats. They are not normally dependent
on humans for food and shelter.

LION
The lion is a large wild cat. Lions mainly live in grasslands and savannas.
They are carnivores and usually eat animals such as antelope and zebra.
Female lions are called lionesses and young lions are called cubs.

TIGER
The tiger is the largest living cat species. Tigers are carnivores and mainly
hunt animals such as deer and wild boar. Tigers usually live alone and are
strong swimmers.

ELEPHANT
The elephant is the largest land animal. Elephants are herbivores and eat
grass, leaves, bark, fruits, and roots. They use their trunks for breathing,
smelling, drinking, and picking up objects.

GIRAFFE
The giraffe is the tallest living land animal. It has a long neck and long
legs. Giraffes are herbivores and mainly eat leaves from trees.

ZEBRA
The zebra is a herbivorous animal known for its black-and-white striped coat.
Zebras mainly live in African grasslands.

BEAR
Bears are large mammals with strong bodies and thick fur. Different bear
species eat different foods. Many bears are omnivores, while polar bears are
mainly carnivorous.

DEER
Deer are herbivorous mammals that usually live in forests, grasslands, and
woodlands. Male deer of many species grow antlers.

MONKEY
Monkeys are primates. They are intelligent, social animals and many species
live in forests. Their diet may include fruits, leaves, seeds, insects, and
small animals.

============================================================
4. PET ANIMALS
============================================================

Pet animals are animals kept by humans mainly for companionship, enjoyment,
or assistance.

DOG
Dogs are one of the most common pet animals. They are mammals and have a
strong sense of smell and hearing. Dogs can be trained for companionship,
security, search and rescue, guiding people, and many other tasks.

CAT
Cats are popular pet mammals. They are agile, have sharp claws, and have
excellent night vision. Domestic cats usually eat animal-based foods.

RABBIT
Rabbits are small herbivorous mammals. They eat foods such as grass, hay,
leafy vegetables, and suitable pellets. Rabbits have strong hind legs and long
ears.

HAMSTER
Hamsters are small rodents commonly kept as pets. They are active animals and
often store food in cheek pouches.

GUINEA PIG
Guinea pigs are small herbivorous rodents. They are social animals and are
commonly kept as pets.

PARROT
Parrots are colorful birds often kept as pets. Many parrots can imitate sounds
and human speech. They have strong curved beaks.

BUDGERIGAR
The budgerigar, also called a budgie, is a small parrot species commonly kept
as a pet bird.

GOLDFISH
Goldfish are freshwater fish commonly kept as pets in aquariums or suitable
ponds.

============================================================
5. DOMESTIC AND FARM ANIMALS
============================================================

Domestic animals are animals that humans have bred and kept for food, work,
companionship, transport, or other purposes.

COW
Cows are herbivorous mammals. They eat grass and other plant-based feed. Cows
are commonly raised for milk and, in some regions, meat.

BUFFALO
Buffalo are large herbivorous mammals. Domestic water buffalo are used for
milk, farming, and transport in many parts of the world.

GOAT
Goats are herbivorous mammals. They are commonly raised for milk, meat,
fiber, and other agricultural purposes.

SHEEP
Sheep are herbivorous mammals. They are often raised for wool, milk, and meat.

HORSE
Horses are large herbivorous mammals. They have been used for transport,
agriculture, sport, and recreation.

DONKEY
Donkeys are herbivorous mammals closely related to horses. They have long
been used to carry loads and assist with farm work.

CHICKEN
Chickens are domestic birds. They are commonly raised for eggs and meat.

DUCK
Ducks are water birds that may be kept on farms for eggs and meat.

============================================================
6. BIRDS
============================================================

Birds are warm-blooded vertebrates with feathers, beaks, and wings. Birds lay
eggs. Most birds can fly, but some birds such as ostriches and penguins cannot
fly.

EAGLE
Eagles are large birds of prey. They have strong talons, sharp beaks, and
excellent eyesight.

SPARROW
Sparrows are small birds commonly found near human settlements. Many sparrows
eat seeds and insects.

PARROT
Parrots are colorful birds with curved beaks. They are intelligent and many
species can imitate sounds.

PEACOCK
A peacock is the male of the Indian peafowl. It is known for its large,
colorful tail feathers, which it displays during courtship.

OWL
Owls are birds of prey. Many owl species are active at night. They have
excellent hearing and vision adapted for hunting.

PIGEON
Pigeons are birds commonly found in cities and towns. They can navigate over
long distances and have historically been used to carry messages.

CROW
Crows are highly intelligent birds. They are omnivores and can eat fruits,
seeds, insects, carrion, and human food waste.

OSTRICH
The ostrich is the largest living bird. It cannot fly, but it can run at high
speed.

PENGUIN
Penguins are flightless birds adapted to aquatic life. They are excellent
swimmers and mainly live in the Southern Hemisphere.

KINGFISHER
Kingfishers are birds known for their bright colors and ability to catch fish
and other small aquatic animals.

============================================================
7. WATER ANIMALS
============================================================

Water animals live mainly or entirely in aquatic environments such as oceans,
seas, rivers, lakes, ponds, and wetlands.

FISH
Fish are aquatic vertebrates that usually breathe through gills and move using
fins.

DOLPHIN
Dolphins are aquatic mammals. They breathe air through lungs and must come to
the surface to breathe. Dolphins are intelligent and social animals.

WHALE
Whales are marine mammals. They breathe air using lungs and come to the
surface to breathe. The blue whale is the largest animal known to have lived
on Earth.

SHARK
Sharks are fish with skeletons made mostly of cartilage rather than bone.
Most sharks are predators, though their diets vary by species.

OCTOPUS
Octopuses are marine mollusks with eight arms. They are intelligent and can
change color and texture for camouflage and communication.

SQUID
Squid are marine mollusks. They have a soft body, arms, tentacles, and can
move quickly by jet propulsion.

JELLYFISH
Jellyfish are aquatic invertebrates with soft, gelatinous bodies. Many species
have tentacles containing stinging cells.

SEA TURTLE
Sea turtles are marine reptiles. They breathe air and come ashore mainly to
lay eggs.

CRAB
Crabs are crustaceans with hard exoskeletons and usually five pairs of legs.

LOBSTER
Lobsters are marine crustaceans with hard exoskeletons and large claws in many
species.

SEAHORSE
Seahorses are small marine fish with horse-like heads. In seahorses, the male
carries developing young in a brood pouch.

STARFISH
Starfish, also called sea stars, are marine invertebrates. Most have five arms,
although some species have more.

============================================================
8. FRESHWATER ANIMALS
============================================================

Freshwater animals live in rivers, lakes, ponds, streams, and wetlands.

Examples:
- Goldfish
- Carp
- Catfish
- Trout
- Freshwater turtle
- Frog
- Crocodile
- River dolphin
- Freshwater crab

FROG
Frogs are amphibians. They usually begin life in water as tadpoles and later
develop into adults with legs.

CROCODILE
Crocodiles are large reptiles that live near rivers, lakes, wetlands, and
estuaries. They are carnivores.

============================================================
9. REPTILES
============================================================

Reptiles are vertebrate animals with scales or scaly skin. Most reptiles lay
eggs, although some species give birth to live young.

SNAKE
Snakes are legless reptiles. Different snake species may eat rodents, birds,
fish, insects, eggs, or other animals.

LIZARD
Lizards are reptiles found in many habitats. Most have four legs, although
some species are legless.

CROCODILE
Crocodiles are large semi-aquatic reptiles with powerful jaws.

TURTLE
Turtles are reptiles protected by shells. Different species live on land,
in freshwater, or in the ocean.

============================================================
10. AMPHIBIANS
============================================================

Amphibians are vertebrates that usually spend part of their life in water and
part on land.

Examples:
- Frogs
- Toads
- Salamanders
- Newts

Frogs usually hatch from eggs as tadpoles. Tadpoles live in water and later
change into adult frogs through a process called metamorphosis.

============================================================
11. INSECTS
============================================================

Insects are invertebrates with six legs and three main body sections:
head, thorax, and abdomen.

BUTTERFLY
Butterflies are insects with large wings. They develop through four main life
stages: egg, larva, pupa, and adult.

BEE
Bees are insects important for pollination. Many bee species collect nectar
and pollen from flowers.

ANT
Ants are social insects that live in colonies. Colonies may contain queens,
workers, and other specialized members.

MOSQUITO
Mosquitoes are small flying insects. Female mosquitoes of many species feed on
blood because they need nutrients for egg development.

DRAGONFLY
Dragonflies are flying insects often found near water. Their young stages,
called nymphs, live in water.

============================================================
12. ARACHNIDS
============================================================

Arachnids are invertebrates with eight legs.

Examples:
- Spiders
- Scorpions
- Ticks
- Mites

SPIDER
Spiders are arachnids, not insects. Most spiders produce silk and many use it
to build webs.

SCORPION
Scorpions are arachnids with pincers and a segmented tail containing a sting.

============================================================
13. ANIMAL DIETS
============================================================

Animals can be grouped according to what they eat.

HERBIVORES
Herbivores mainly eat plants.

Examples:
- Cow
- Horse
- Elephant
- Deer
- Rabbit
- Giraffe
- Goat

CARNIVORES
Carnivores mainly eat other animals.

Examples:
- Lion
- Tiger
- Shark
- Crocodile
- Eagle

OMNIVORES
Omnivores eat both plant and animal matter.

Examples:
- Bear
- Crow
- Pig
- Many monkeys
- Humans

============================================================
18. ENDANGERED ANIMALS
============================================================

An endangered species is a species at serious risk of extinction.

Causes of endangerment can include:
- Habitat destruction
- Illegal hunting
- Pollution
- Climate change
- Overfishing
- Invasive species

Examples of species that have faced serious conservation threats include:
- Tigers
- Asian elephants
- Many rhinoceros species
- Sea turtles
- Some whale species
- Some orangutan species

Conservation efforts include protecting habitats, reducing illegal wildlife
trade, controlling pollution, and supporting breeding and recovery programs.

============================================================
19. PET CARE BASICS
============================================================

Pet animals need responsible care.

Important needs include:
- Clean drinking water
- Suitable food
- Safe shelter
- Exercise
- Hygiene
- Veterinary care
- Vaccination when recommended
- Appropriate social interaction
- A clean environment

Different species have different care requirements. Owners should follow
veterinary and species-specific guidance.
